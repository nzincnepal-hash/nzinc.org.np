About foundation

Our Vision :

Well recognized, learning and innovative institution for community plus humane development

NZINC (Nepal Zebrafish in Nature Center) is a non-governmental, non-political and non-profit making research and social development organization. Established with a commitment to scientific excellence and ecological preservation, NZINC aims to be recognized as a capable and committed national level organization dedicated to biodiversity conservation, sustainable agriculture, and humane development.

Mission

Mission of our
foundation:

NZINC is committed to be connector for accessing resources for the right of people

NZINC works in partnership with a range of local, national and international, government and non-government, private sectors and academic institutions. It maintains active research affiliations with Agriculture and Forestry University, Mid-West University, Far-West University, DUKE University, University of Oregon, Social Welfare Council, and Climate Adaptation Networks across Nepal.

Why Community?

Empowering Communities for Sustainable Development

Empowering Local Solutions

Communities best understand their challenges. Supporting them fosters localized, sustainable solutions that address their unique needs effectively.

Building Resilience

Strong communities can adapt to adversity, recover from setbacks, and sustain growth, ensuring long-term stability and progress.

Reducing Inequality

Community support bridges gaps in access to education, healthcare, and resources, promoting equal opportunities for all members.

Fostering Collaboration

Encouraging community-led initiatives strengthens bonds among individuals, fostering unity, trust, and shared responsibility for growth.

Target Group

Empowering Communities,
Transforming Lives

NZINC is dedicated to uplifting marginalized and vulnerable groups, fostering inclusion and opportunity.

We focus on improving the lives of Women, Seniors, Discriminated People, Youth, Children.

Women

Promoting gender equality through education, empowerment, and economic independence.

Seniors

Ensuring dignity, care, and active participation in community life.

Discriminated People

Advocating for social justice and equal opportunities to combat discrimination.

Youth

Equipping young individuals with skills, knowledge, and opportunities for a brighter future.

Children

Enhancing access to education, healthcare, and safety for holistic development.
