Become a volunteer

Volunteering with the Nepal Zebrafish in Nature Center (NZINC) means directly driving community empowerment and aquatic biodiversity protection. As a volunteer, you will contribute your skills across nature restoration, educational outreach, river basin field research, and sustainable livelihood programs across Nepal.

Why Volunteer at NZINC?

Make a Difference with NZINC

Joining NZINC connects you with a passionate team working tirelessly to safeguard freshwater ecosystems and foster community resilience. Your time directly powers critical field initiatives and environmental education.

Gain practical experience in social development, biodiversity research, and grassroots action while collaborating with dedicated experts.

Connect with professional volunteers and researchers worldwide.
Help riverbank communities enhance their environmental quality of life.
Broaden your field research and project management skill set.
Be part of vital nature restoration and ecological preservation initiatives.

We need your voice, join us!

Get in touch to join our upcoming field programs. Our team typically responds within six business days.
