Research Studies

Advancing Zebrafish Research, Biodiversity Conservation and Freshwater Ecosystem Sustainability

NZINC conducts research to advance understanding of native zebrafish populations, their natural habitats, and their importance to scientific research and aquatic biodiversity conservation. Our publications highlight zebrafish as a model organism, the ecological and genetic significance of wild populations, and Nepal's role in zebrafish science. Through evidence-based research and institutional collaboration, NZINC aims to strengthen scientific knowledge and support the conservation of freshwater ecosystems.
