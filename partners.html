Our Network

Transforming Lives Through Strategic Partnerships

Collaborating with NGOs and INGOs for Rural and Social Development

Since its inception, NZINC has recognized the power of partnerships in achieving sustainable development goals. By collaborating with local, national, and international Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs and INGOs), NZINC has been able to amplify its impact across various sectors, including livelihood enhancement, biodiversity conservation, disaster risk reduction, health, and social empowerment.
